Safe Ride LK — Smart Women & Child Safety Mobility Platform

An Android-based personal safety mobility platform engineered with background shake-to-SOS triggers, real-time geofenced live tracking, automated zero-data SMS dispatch, and local SQLite offline incident caching.

The Problem

Public transit commuters and solo travelers in Sri Lanka often encounter sudden personal safety emergencies or harassment. In critical moments, victims have zero time to unlock a smartphone, navigate multi-step apps, or depend on continuous 4G data coverage in low-signal transport corridors. Existing commercial emergency apps either require constant active screen interaction or fail completely when mobile data is dropped.

The Solution

I engineered Safe Ride LK as a low-latency native Android mobile client using Kotlin and Java. It operates a background accelerometer sensor listener capable of recognizing vigorous shaking gestures even when the device is locked in a pocket. The system instantly compiles GPS coordinates and fires automated SMS dispatch messages to pre-configured trusted contacts without requiring active mobile data.

Key Technical Features

Hardware Shake-to-SOS

Monitors accelerometer G-force differential (threshold > 12m/s²) across X, Y, and Z axes to differentiate purposeful emergency shaking from natural walking or pocket movement.

Real-Time GPS Geofencing

Interfaces Google Play LocationServices FusedLocationProviderClient to stream high-accuracy coordinates and calculate perimeter boundaries around common transit routes.

Zero-Data SMS Dispatch

Direct hardware telephony broadcast sends GSM SMS messages embedding Google Maps tracking links even when cellular 4G/LTE mobile data is completely severed.

Offline SQLite Incident Caching

Local ACID-compliant SQLite relational tables preserve emergency contact hashes, emergency event timestamps, and transit logs with zero external server dependencies.

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1: Android Doze Mode & Background Sensor Termination

Starting with Android 8.0 (API 26) and tightened in Android 12+, the Android OS aggressively suspends background services to conserve battery life, killing the accelerometer sensor listener when the device screen locks.

Solution: Implemented a high-priority Android Foreground Service linked to an ongoing low-profile notification channel. Leveraged partial WakeLocks only during active sensor threshold evaluation loops and registered a BroadcastReceiver to rebind services upon device restart.

Challenge 2: Preventing False Positive SOS Triggers

Jogging, cycling, or bumping into objects can induce transient acceleration spikes that might accidentally trigger emergency alerts to family members.

Solution: Developed a time-windowed hysteresis algorithm. The system requires at least 3 distinct acceleration vector reversals exceeding the 12m/s² threshold within a strict 1500ms sliding window before initiating the emergency sequence.
